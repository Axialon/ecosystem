> **Balanc'em** is a zero-build, offline-first 3D WebGL spatial computing engine and combat meta balancer for game designers, combat leads, and RPG systems architects.

---

## 💎 Mathematical Model: Combat Meta Law

$$N_{DPS} \cdot N_{EHP} = K \cdot (N_{Cooldown} \cdot N_{APM})$$

Where:
- $N_{DPS}$ = Damage Per Second burst potential
- $N_{EHP}$ = Effective Health Pool (Hit Points + Armor Mitigation)
- $N_{Cooldown}$ = Resource Friction & Ability Cooldown Costs
- $N_{APM}$ = Execution Ceiling (Actions Per Minute)

---

## 🚀 Quickstart

1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) in your browser to launch the 3D Combat Balancing Solver.
2. Open [`showcase.html`](showcase.html) to explore RPG combat archetypes.
3. Export Unreal Engine / Unity CSV DataTables and 3D `.glb` combat polytopes.
