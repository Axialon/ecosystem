## 💎 Mathematical Model: The 6-Pillar FinOps Law

Orbit'em projects cloud trade-offs onto a 6-node spatial topology governed by the invariant:

$$N_A \cdot N_P = K \cdot N_C \cdot N_\Omega$$

Where:
- $N_A$ = Availability SLA Target (e.g. 99.999% "Five Nines")
- $N_P$ = Latency Performance Threshold (p99 latency in ms)
- $N_C$ = Monthly FinOps Cloud Budget ($/mo)
- $N_\Omega$ = Operational Complexity & Blast Radius

When an engineer pulls the **SLA Target** higher, the central crystalline reactor expands dynamically along the Y-axis, exerting reciprocal tensile force that demands increased compute redundancy ($N_{vCPU}$) or budget allocation ($N_C$).

## 🚀 Quickstart

Orbit'em is completely standalone and requires **zero dependencies or build steps**.

1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) in your browser to launch the Power Tool.
2. Open [`showcase.html`](showcase.html) to view the interactive marketing presentation.
3. Export validated `.orbit.json` configs, standalone 3D binary `.glb` models, and Terraform `.tf` blueprints with 1 click.
