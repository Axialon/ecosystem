> **Cap'em** is a zero-build, offline-first 3D venture finance computing engine that models startup cap table waterfalls, SAFE note stacking, ESOP option pool expansions, and founder dilution in real-time 3D.

---

## 💎 Mathematical Invariant: Startup Dilution Law

$$N_E \cdot N_V = K \cdot N_R \cdot N_C$$

Where:
- $N_E$ = Founder Common Equity Retained (%)
- $N_V$ = Post-Money Enterprise Valuation ($)
- $N_R$ = Cash Runway Duration (Months)
- $N_C$ = Capital Raised ($)

---

## 🚀 Quickstart

1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) in your browser to launch the 3D Cap Table Solver.
2. Open [`showcase.html`](showcase.html) for venture scenario modeling.
3. Export Carta-compatible CSVs, `.capbox.json` schemas, and pitch deck embeds.
