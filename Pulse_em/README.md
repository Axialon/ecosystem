> **Pulse'em** is a zero-telemetry, offline-first 3D biometric computing engine that models physiological trade-offs between athletic training strain, autonomic HRV sleep recovery, metabolic fueling, and cellular autophagy clearing.

---

## 💎 Mathematical Model: 5-Vector Longevity Law

$$N_R \cdot N_L = K \cdot N_S \cdot N_E$$

Where:
- $N_R$ = Autonomic Recovery (rMSSD HRV in ms)
- $N_L$ = Biological Longevity Multiplier
- $N_S$ = Weekly Training Strain (TRIMP score)
- $N_E$ = Metabolic Fueling Balance (%)

When an athlete raises **Training Strain** ($N_S$), the cellular core elongates and requires compensatory **Autonomic Recovery** ($N_R$) or **Autophagy Clearance** ($N_A$) to prevent accelerated biological aging.
